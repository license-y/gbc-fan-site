**Green Beans Coffee in Kitasando, Tokyo, welcomes dogs at every indoor seat, which makes it an easy, low-stress place to mark your dog's birthday without worrying about weather or finding somewhere that'll actually let your dog in.**

A dog's birthday doesn't have to mean a party at home. Stepping out to somewhere your dog is genuinely welcome, not just tolerated, can make the day feel a little more special, and that's part of why I keep coming back here with my own dog, Pon.

## Why a Cafe Birthday Works

Every seat at Green Beans Coffee is indoors and pet-friendly, so you're not stuck hoping for good weather or settling for a terrace table. You can order a coffee, something from the gluten-free menu, and just let your dog relax beside you for however long you want to stay — no countdown, no rush to free up the table for the next booking.

## What's Actually Confirmed

I want to be upfront about what I know for sure versus what I don't, since birthday-specific offers can change.

- **A dog birthday has happened here before.** When my own dog Pon turned 2, the cafe hosted what we ended up calling "Pon's Birthday Festival," and a number of other dogs came by to join in. It was a one-off event tied to my own visit, not a standing monthly thing, so I can't promise the same setup will happen for your dog's birthday.
- **A cheki photo is a nice way to mark the day.** When the cafe isn't too busy, staff will take a free instant cheki photo of your dog, which gets added to the photo wall near the counter. It's a good way to have *something* to remember the visit by, even though the photo itself stays at the cafe rather than coming home with you (see below).
- **I haven't been able to confirm a dog birthday cake, treat, or package.** I'm not going to say one exists here, because I haven't seen it offered. If this matters to you, it's worth asking staff directly or messaging the official [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/green.beanscoffee) before you visit.

## A Photo to Remember the Day — Without Taking It Home

One thing worth knowing in advance: cheki photos at Green Beans Coffee are a display-only service. Staff take the photo and add it to the growing wall of pet portraits near the counter, but prints aren't given out to take home. If a physical keepsake matters to you, it's worth bringing your own camera or phone for photos you can keep, and treating the cheki as something you can go back and visit on the wall rather than something you'll walk out with. [Cheki Pet Photos in Tokyo](/en/pets/articles/pet-photo-experience-tokyo/) covers the full details, including why it isn't guaranteed on every visit.

## Celebrating as a Group

If you're hoping to celebrate with other dog owners rather than just your own table, the cafe can be booked for private hire, with capacity for up to 20 seated or 30 standing. Reservations for this are typically accepted anywhere from three months out to two weeks ahead, so it's worth planning further in advance than a casual visit. This isn't a birthday-specific package — it's the same private hire option available for any kind of group booking — but it's a realistic way to turn a dog's birthday into more of an event if that's what you're after. It's best to ask about this directly through [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/green.beanscoffee) or by phone.

## Tips for the Day

- **Go outside the lunch rush** if you want a better chance at the cheki photo, since it depends on staff having a quiet moment to take it
- **Let your dog settle in before the cake talk (or candle) comes out** — a calmer dog makes for a calmer photo, if you do end up by the counter for one
- **Bring your own camera for keepsake photos**, since the cheki stays at the cafe
- **Message ahead on Instagram** if a specific date matters to you, whether that's for the cheki photo or just to check how busy things look

## Getting There

Green Beans Coffee is a 3-minute walk from Kitasando Station or a 7-minute walk from Sendagaya Station, in the Dagayasando area of Shibuya, Tokyo — about 10 minutes from Shinjuku.
