If you're planning a Tokyo trip with a dog that rides in a stroller rather than walks the whole way, it's worth knowing in advance which cafes actually work with that setup. Here's what's generally true of pet-friendly cafes in Tokyo, and what's specifically confirmed at Green Beans Coffee.

## Can You Bring a Dog Stroller Into a Cafe in Tokyo?

In general, yes, at cafes that describe themselves as pet-friendly — but it's common for these cafes to ask that a dog stay in a carrier, stroller, or on a short leash rather than roam freely, especially once you're seated near other guests. This is a fairly typical expectation across pet-friendly dining in Japan, not something unique to any one shop, and it's worth treating it as the default assumption until you confirm otherwise at a specific cafe.

## What to Check Before Visiting With a Dog Stroller

A few things are worth confirming before you go, since policies vary by cafe:

- **Indoor vs. terrace-only access** — some "pet-friendly" cafes only mean an outdoor table, which matters more if you're relying on a stroller to get around
- **Whether the stroller needs to stay closed** at the table, or if there's room to park it open beside you
- **Aisle and seating width** — a larger stroller can be harder to maneuver in a small, densely seated room
- **Any size or number limits**, which some cafes set but don't always publish online

## Dog Strollers at Green Beans Coffee in Kitasando

Here's what's actually confirmed for Green Beans Coffee specifically: every seat indoors is open to dogs of any breed and size, including large dogs, and the cafe asks that dogs stay on a leash or be kept in a carrier or stroller while inside. Dogs aren't permitted on the tables themselves, for hygiene reasons.

A few specifics aren't listed anywhere official — stroller size or number limits, whether there's a spot set aside for parking one, and whether the cafe lends strollers out. If any of that matters for your visit — a double stroller, for instance, or a particularly large cart — it's worth sending a quick message to staff via [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/green.beanscoffee) before you go.

## Why a Comfortable Indoor Cafe Matters

Tokyo's weather swings between humid summers and cold winters, and a stroller parked on an outdoor terrace in either season is a rougher ride than one parked beside an indoor table. Having a genuinely indoor option near Kitasando and Sendagaya — rather than a terrace-only "pet-friendly" label — matters more than it sounds like on paper, especially on a day when you're already managing a stroller, a dog, and your own coffee.
