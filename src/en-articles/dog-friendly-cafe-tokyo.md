**Green Beans Coffee in Kitasando, Shibuya, is a fully pet-friendly cafe — every seat welcomes dogs indoors, including large breeds, with no separate pet-only section.**

Finding a Tokyo cafe where you don't have to leave your dog outside, or squeeze into a small designated pet corner, can take some searching. This one's layout makes it simple: the whole room is pet-friendly, so you and your dog can sit wherever there's space.

## Why Indoor Pet-Friendly Matters in Tokyo

Tokyo's weather swings between humid summers and cold winters, and a lot of "pet-friendly" cafes in the city only mean an outdoor terrace. That's not much use on a rainy day or a 35-degree afternoon. Having a genuinely indoor, air-conditioned option near Sendagaya and Kitasando — not far from Shinjuku or Harajuku — makes a bigger difference than it might sound.

## What to Expect With Your Dog

Every table at Green Beans Coffee accepts dogs, and the staff keep water bowls on loan for anyone who didn't bring one. I've also noticed a corkboard near the counter filled with instant photos of pets who have visited — a small, ongoing record of regulars' dogs that I always check for new faces. The cafe holds pet photo sessions from time to time as well, so it's worth asking at the counter or checking their Instagram if that's something you'd like to join.

Because the space is quiet rather than bustling, dogs that get nervous around crowds tend to settle in fairly quickly. I've brought first-time visitors whose dogs were initially restless, and within ten minutes or so they'd usually found a comfortable spot under the table.

## Who This Is For

This suits owners who want a real sit-down coffee break with their dog rather than a quick takeaway, including people walking dogs around the Dagayasando area or visiting the nearby National Olympic Stadium and Meiji Jingu area who want a calm stop afterward.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. Can I bring my dog inside a cafe in Shibuya?**<br>
Yes. Green Beans Coffee in Kitasando is fully pet-friendly indoors, with every seat open to dogs of any size, not just a separate terrace area.

**Q. Does the cafe provide anything for pets?**<br>
Water bowls are available on loan, and the shop occasionally hosts pet photo sessions. Staff display instant photos of visiting pets near the counter.

**Q. Is it close to Shinjuku or Harajuku?**<br>
Kitasando sits between the two, a short walk from Sendagaya and roughly ten minutes from Shinjuku, making it an easy stop during a day exploring that part of Tokyo.

## Conclusion

For a dog-friendly cafe in Tokyo that doesn't make you choose between comfort and a proper coffee, Green Beans Coffee in Kitasando is worth the detour.
